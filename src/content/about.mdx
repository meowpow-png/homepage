I usually introduce myself as a software developer. My wife insists that “software engineer” is a
title reserved for people who attended fancy engineering schools. Since she’s usually right, I’ve
stopped arguing.

Lately I’ve been spending a lot of time exploring AI-assisted software development, backend
systems, and developer tooling. I don’t see AI as a replacement for developers—I see it as an
excuse to build even more ambitious things.

Here you’ll find things I’ve built, things I’m currently obsessed with, and technical
documentation pretending to be blog posts.
