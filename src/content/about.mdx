I usually introduce myself as a software developer. My wife insists that “software engineer”
is a title reserved for people who went to fancy engineering schools. Since she’s
usually right, I’ve stopped arguing.

Lately I’ve been spending a lot of time exploring AI-assisted software development,
backend systems, and developer tooling. Unfortunately, AI hasn't made me a better developer,
it has just made my bad ideas cheaper to try.

Here you’ll find things I’ve built, things I’m currently obsessed with,
and technical documentation pretending to be blog posts.
